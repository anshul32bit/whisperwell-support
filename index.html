Account Deletion Request for WhisperWell

We are sorry to see you go. If you wish to permanently delete your WhisperWell account and all associated data, please follow the instructions below.

Instructions:

To request account deletion, you must send an email from the address associated with your WhisperWell account.

  1. Compose a new email to our support address: afterhoursofworks@gmail.com
  2. Use the subject line: WhisperWell: Account Deletion Request
  3. In the body of the email, please state clearly that you wish to delete your account.

What Happens Next?

Once we receive your request, we will verify that it came from the registered email address. After verification, we will permanently delete your account and all of your data, including diary entries and questionnaire responses, from our servers within 14 business days. We will notify you by email once the deletion is complete.

Please note: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
